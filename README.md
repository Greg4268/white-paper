# Rethinking the Adoption of AI Surveillance Infrastructure: An Information Governance Failure 

This paper analyzes how cities' rapid adoption of AI-powered ALPR surveillance systems without robust governance creates preventable security, privacy, and legal risks. Drawing on documented abuses, technical vulnerabilities, and policy failures, this paper outlines a governance-first roadmap using COBIT and ISO 27001 controls to guide municipal technology leaders in securely and ethically deploying or reassessing these systems.
